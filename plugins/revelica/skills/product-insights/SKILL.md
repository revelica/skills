---
name: product-insights
description: "Find where you can win: opportunities customers feel that today's options serve poorly, so you can build something differentiated and hard to copy. Customer centric: research the market only as far as it explains the customer. Covers who the customer is and the job they're trying to get done, what sets them looking, what holds them back, the steps of their workflow and where it hurts, the words they use, and how well the products and workarounds they use today handle each step (competitive analysis: a step-by-step teardown of each product as a story map with screenshots, your own as the baseline). Use when getting to know a new customer or product category quickly, doing competitive analysis or market research, analyzing an interview or survey, checking how a competitor handles a flow before building your own, or scoping feature parity. Tests and experiments produce insights too. Gives you a researched customer journey, today's options rated at each step, and the opportunities where there's room to win."
---

Research our customer and the products they use today, and show where there's room to win.
