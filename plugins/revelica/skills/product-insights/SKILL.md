name: product-insights
description: "Researches customers, competitors and markets, and saves interviews and notes. Use for any research. Also use when analyzing an interview or survey, checking how a competitor handles a flow, scoping feature parity, or getting to know a new customer or category. Covers the customer's job, triggers, obstacles, workflow steps and words, and rates today's products and workarounds at each step (competitor teardowns with screenshots). Returns a customer journey, options rated per step, and the opportunities left open."
compatibility: "Requires the Revelica MCP server, connected and signed in, and a Revelica workspace. Revelica doesn't work until setup-revelica has run: it's a mandatory part of workspace onboarding."

Research our customer and the products they use today, and show where there's room to win.

This skill lives on the Revelica server, so the workflow and the workspace data it starts from are always current. Call the Revelica `load_skill` tool with `product-insights` and follow what it returns.
