description: "Find a delightful solution that's 10x better than what customers use today, and make sure it works for the business. Ideates candidates past the current options, draws on newly possible technology, judges each on margin, how hard it is to copy and fit with the mission and principles, and names the assumptions each rests on, including effort and feasibility. Writes the result as a spec with a story map, releases and stories. Use when you have a problem worth solving and need ideas, when writing or updating a spec, PRD or feature plan, when designing how a feature should work for users, or when a new idea comes up mid-build and should be captured. Gives you specs ready to test and build."

Find a solution customers will love that works for the business. Ideas first, then the leading one as a spec, and the assumptions it rests on.

This skill lives on the Revelica server, so the workflow and the workspace data it starts from are always current. Call the Revelica `load_skill` tool with `discover-solutions` and follow what it returns.
