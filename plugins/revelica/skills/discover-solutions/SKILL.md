---
name: discover-solutions
description: "Generates solution ideas and writes specs and PRDs. Use when designing or specifying a feature. Also use when a problem needs ideas, a spec needs updating, or an idea comes up mid-build. Judges each idea on margin, how hard it is to copy and fit with the strategy, and names the assumptions it rests on, including effort and feasibility. Returns specs with a story map, releases and stories, ready to test and build."
compatibility: "Requires the Revelica MCP server, connected and signed in, and a Revelica workspace. Revelica doesn't work until setup-revelica has run: it's a mandatory part of workspace onboarding."
---

Find a solution customers will love that works for the business. Ideas first, then the leading one as a spec, and the assumptions it rests on.

This skill lives on the Revelica server, so the workflow and the workspace data it starts from are always current. Call the Revelica `load_skill` tool with `discover-solutions` and follow what it returns.
