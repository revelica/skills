Find the riskiest assumptions in this bet or spec and test them with the quickest method that settles each, then make the call. Build, iterate or reconsider the bet.

This skill lives on the Revelica server, so the workflow and the workspace data it starts from are always current. Call the Revelica `load_skill` tool with `test-product-risks` and follow what it returns.
