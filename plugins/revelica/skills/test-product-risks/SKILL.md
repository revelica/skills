name: test-product-risks
description: "Test the risks in a bet and in the solutions you're discovering before you build: will customers value it, can they use it, can we build it, does it work for the business, and is it right to do. Find the holes in an idea now, so bias and blind spots don't lead you to failure. Picks the riskiest assumptions and tests each with the quickest method that settles it: desk research, a one-question survey, data mining, an experiment, an analysis of competing hypotheses, a spike, or a proof of concept or prototype. Use when asking whether something will work or how you'd know, before committing to build, when spiking whether something is technically possible, when building a prototype to try an idea, or when checking whether something that shipped moved the goal. Gives you tested assumptions and a call: build, iterate, or reconsider the bet."
---
