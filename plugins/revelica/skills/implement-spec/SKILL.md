name: implement-spec
description: "Rapidly and incrementally build what discovery found. Picks up the spec's release, its stories and acceptance criteria, and the decisions and research behind them, so you build the right thing without asking again. Use when picking up a feature, ticket or spec to implement, before starting work on something to check what's already known, or while building. As you build, write back what you learn: feasibility and effort as assumptions on the spec (with the spike or test as evidence when you ran one), tradeoffs and limits you hit, new ideas as specs, and what shipped. When the request turns out to be product work rather than code, switch to the matching skill."
---

Help me build this feature. Read its spec, the release and stories in scope, their acceptance criteria, and the decisions and research behind them, then build it and write back what we learn.

This skill lives on the Revelica server, so the workflow and the workspace data it starts from are always current. Call the Revelica `load_skill` tool with `implement-spec` and follow what it returns.
