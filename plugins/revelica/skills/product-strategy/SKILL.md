---
name: product-strategy
description: "Decides which bets to make or drop, and why. Use when asking if something is worth building. Also use when asking what to work on, which of several ideas or requests matters most, or before committing time to a feature. Weighs each opportunity's value to customers, how well it's served today, how much it could move the goal, and what's newly possible. Returns bets compared, the ones declined with reasons, and what evidence would change the call."
compatibility: "Requires the Revelica MCP server, connected and signed in, and a Revelica workspace. Revelica doesn't work until setup-revelica has run: it's a mandatory part of workspace onboarding."
---

Compare the bets we could make toward our goal. Assess the opportunities behind them, recommend which to make and which to decline, say why, and say what evidence would change the call.

This skill lives on the Revelica server, so the workflow and the workspace data it starts from are always current. Call the Revelica `load_skill` tool with `product-strategy` and follow what it returns.
