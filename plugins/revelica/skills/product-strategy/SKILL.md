---
name: product-strategy
description: "Decide what to focus on and what not to: which bets to make, which to decline, and why. A bet says which customer opportunity has the best chance of delivering a goal, and how. Weighs all available insights: how valuable each opportunity is to customers given how well it's served today, how much it could move the goal, where a novel solution could be a big leap, and what's newly possible. Keeps capturing insights as they arrive, always looking around the corner. Use when asking what to work on, whether something is worth pursuing or building at all, which of several ideas or requests matters most, or before committing time to a feature. Gives you bets compared, the ones declined with reasons, and what evidence would change the call."
---

Compare the bets we could make toward our goal. Assess the opportunities behind them, recommend which to make and which to decline, say why, and say what evidence would change the call.

This skill lives on the Revelica server, so the workflow and the workspace data it starts from are always current. Call the Revelica `load_skill` tool with `product-strategy` and follow what it returns.
